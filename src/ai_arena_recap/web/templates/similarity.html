Play-style similarity

How alike do two bots play? For every pair this compares who they beat and lose to, after subtracting what ELO already predicts — so it measures style, not strength. Two bots of equal rating score near zero unless they actually over- and under-perform against the same opponents. How it works.

Read this as similarity, not as an accusation. A high score has plenty of innocent explanations: a shared open-source template, the same standard build order, or simply two bots by the same author. There is only one way to 12-pool, and two bots that both do it will look alike here.
